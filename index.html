Privacy Policy for JobTrack

Effective date: October 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how JobTrack, an Android application for tracking job applications, handles your information.

1. Information You Enter

JobTrack allows you to record job application details such as company name, job title, application status, application date, interview date, and notes. This information is stored locally on your device using the app's local database.

2. Data Collection and Sharing

JobTrack does not operate a developer-controlled server and does not include advertising or analytics services. The app does not send the job application information you enter to the developer.

If you choose to export your applications to a CSV file, the file is saved to a location you select using Android's file picker. Any subsequent sharing of that file is under your control.

3. Notifications and Permissions

JobTrack may use notification and alarm permissions to remind you about upcoming interviews. Notification behavior depends on your device settings and Android version.

4. Data Storage and Security

Your job application records are stored locally on your device. Protecting your device with a screen lock and keeping it secure is recommended. No method of storage is completely secure.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Your records remain on your device until you delete them, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app. Clearing app data or uninstalling may permanently remove locally stored records. Export important information before doing so.

6. Children's Privacy

JobTrack is a job application tracking tool and is not designed specifically for children. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children through a developer-operated service.

7. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when the app's features or data practices change. Any revised policy will be published at this webpage with an updated effective date.
